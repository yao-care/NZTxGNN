# Privacy Policy

<div class="key-takeaway">
This is a static site — no registration required, no personally identifiable data collected.
</div>

---

## What we collect

<p class="key-answer" data-question="Does NZTxGNN collect personal data?">
<strong>No.</strong> This site is hosted on GitHub Pages as a purely static site. There is no
account system, no forms, and no tracking cookies. We do not collect names, email addresses,
medical records or any personally identifiable information.
</p>

---

## Third-party services

The site uses the following third-party resources, which may log access according to their own
policies:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy policy |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| GitHub Pages | Site hosting | [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) |
| d3js.org | Charting library | Static asset CDN |
| giscus | Comments (where enabled) | [giscus](https://giscus.app/) |

When you actively launch the SMART on FHIR app, it requests medication data from your electronic
health record system. That data is <strong>processed entirely within your browser</strong>; it is
never sent to this site's servers and is not stored.

Because this site collects no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or erase.

## About the Developer

This platform is developed and operated by **藥提醒科技有限公司** (yao.care, company registration
number 83620786, 12F, No. 220, Sec. 2, Taiwan Blvd., West Dist., Taichung City, Taiwan).

NZTxGNN is the New Zealand site of the company's "TxGNN Drug Repurposing" product line.
The same system is deployed across 30 countries and regions, each named `{CC}TxGNN`
(JpTxGNN, UsTxGNN, DETxGNN, and so on) at `{cc}txgnn.yao.care`.
Product overview: <https://www.yao.care/medical/txgnn/>.

The TxGNN model itself was developed by the Zitnik Lab at Harvard Medical School and published
in *Nature Medicine*. This platform is the production system 藥提醒科技有限公司 built on top of that
model, covering national drug-registration data integration, dual knowledge-graph and
deep-learning prediction, PubMed / ClinicalTrials evidence grading, and SMART on FHIR
electronic health record integration.
