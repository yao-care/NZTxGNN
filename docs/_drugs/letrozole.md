## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Letrozole belongs to the aromatase inhibitor class. Drugs of this class lower oestrogen levels, which can slow the growth of hormone receptor-positive breast tumours.

Nipple carcinoma and accessory breast carcinoma arise in breast tissue. Aromatase inhibition is therefore plausible if the tumour is hormone receptor positive. This is a theoretical link only. The publications found are case reports and reviews that describe the disease. They do not evaluate letrozole as a treatment. The high score probably reflects the close relationship between nipple carcinoma and breast carcinoma in the model, not disease-specific evidence.
