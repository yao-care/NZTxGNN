## Tocilizumab: Ankylosing spondylitis
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Tocilizumab is an IL-6 receptor antibody originally used for rheumatoid arthritis, juvenile idiopathic arthritis, giant cell (temporal) arteritis and Castleman disease.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **ankylosing spondylitis**, but the supporting evidence is weak: **2 Phase 3 trials were terminated**, and the 18 retrieved publications are mostly reviews, with only one trial-based analysis (BUILDER-1/2) and one case report directly on tocilizumab in ankylosing spondylitis.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, tocilizumab is a humanized monoclonal antibody that blocks the interleukin-6 receptor, and its efficacy in rheumatoid arthritis and juvenile idiopathic arthritis is established.

IL-6 signalling contributes to inflammation and bone remodelling in axial spondyloarthritis, so blocking the IL-6 receptor is biologically plausible. Ankylosing spondylitis shares inflammatory pathways with the arthritides for which tocilizumab is already used.

However, plausibility has not translated into demonstrated efficacy. The two Phase 3 randomized trials in ankylosing spondylitis were terminated, and TNF and IL-17 inhibitors remain the standard of care. Other predicted indications in the data compiled for this report are weaker (for example rheumatoid vasculitis, L4, supported only by case reports) or unsupported (for example hypermobility of coccyx, Kummell disease, and the IL12B-deficiency mycobacterial susceptibility disorder, all L5). Polyarticular juvenile idiopathic arthritis (L1) is effectively an on-label use rather than true repurposing.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were identified. Notable examples include a **Major** interaction with deferiprone, and **Moderate** interactions with warfarin, apixaban, rivaroxaban, dicoumarol, simvastatin, rosuvastatin, hydroxychloroquine, chloroquine, metronidazole, tinidazole, secnidazole, benznidazole, naltrexone, disulfiram and lidocaine. Zinc salts are **Minor**.
- **Additional concerns from the literature**: tocilizumab is associated with serious infection and tuberculosis risk, liver injury (a case of severe liver damage has been reported), and rare paradoxical vasculitis reactions.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only controlled data in ankylosing spondylitis come from two terminated Phase 3 trials with no demonstrated efficacy, and established TNF and IL-17 inhibitors are available.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Full results and termination reasons for NCT01209689 and NCT01209702, including the BUILDER-1/2 outcomes
- Mechanism of action data and the Medsafe package insert warnings and contraindications
- Clarification of regulatory status in New Zealand, since all 4 authorizations are valid
- A comparison against established AS therapies to show a distinct clinical role
