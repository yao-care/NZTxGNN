## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, roxithromycin is a macrolide antibiotic that inhibits bacterial protein synthesis by binding the 50S ribosomal subunit. Its efficacy in bacterial infections is established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to leprosy, which is caused by *Mycobacterium leprae*.

Several independent laboratory and animal studies support this link. In a mouse footpad model, roxithromycin and clarithromycin were consistently active and bactericidal against *M. leprae*, whereas erythromycin and azithromycin were inactive. Clarithromycin was superior to roxithromycin, and roxithromycin-specific data are limited and appear weaker. A Japanese review also notes that roxithromycin has anti-inflammatory and immunomodulatory activity, which could be relevant to leprosy-related nerve inflammation.

No human trial data were available. The evidence is therefore indirect and preclinical, and clarithromycin is a stronger candidate within the class.

Other predicted indications (for example hypertrichosis, congenital malformation syndromes, nephrogenic syndrome of inappropriate antidiuresis and pulmonary hypertension) have no plausible mechanistic link and no or only indirect supporting evidence. They are model predictions only and are not recommended for further pursuit at this stage.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: The pharmacology data list roxithromycin as a ligand of the motilin receptor (MLNR), a target-level interaction rather than a drug-drug interaction. No specific interacting drugs were identified.

Please refer to the package insert for further safety information, including warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Support for leprosy rests on in vitro and mouse studies plus reviews, with no human trials. Roxithromycin appears less active than clarithromycin, and it has no currently valid licence in New Zealand.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications (currently unavailable) for a safety screen
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Head-to-head comparison with clarithromycin, or human clinical data, to justify choosing roxithromycin
- Clarification of the regulatory pathway, given that both New Zealand licences are cancelled
