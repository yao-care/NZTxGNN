## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data was not available in the data compiled for this report. Captopril belongs to the ACE inhibitor class, which blocks the formation of angiotensin II, a key driver of vasoconstriction and blood-pressure regulation in the renin-angiotensin system (RAS).

Activation of the RAS is thought to contribute to the vascular and kidney damage seen in malignant hypertensive nephrosclerosis. Blocking this pathway is therefore mechanistically plausible. This is a theoretical rationale only. The high TxGNN score is not backed by clinical data, and no study retrieved for this report tests captopril as a treatment for this disease.
