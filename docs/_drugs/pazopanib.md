# Pazopanib: From Renal Cell Carcinoma and Soft Tissue Sarcoma to Liposarcoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Pazopanib is a multi-target tyrosine kinase inhibitor originally used for renal cell carcinoma and non-adipocytic soft tissue sarcoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **Liposarcoma**. This is the highest-ranked prediction with clinical support, and it is backed by **6 clinical trials** and **20 publications**, mostly small Phase 2 studies and reviews.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, pazopanib is a multi-target inhibitor of VEGFR, PDGFR and c-Kit. It is established in renal cell carcinoma and non-adipocytic soft tissue sarcoma, and mechanistically it may be applicable to liposarcoma.

Liposarcoma is an adipocytic soft tissue sarcoma. Adipocytic histology was excluded from the pivotal soft tissue sarcoma trial, so liposarcoma is not covered by the approved sarcoma use. Several Phase 2 trials have tested pazopanib in liposarcoma, but activity appeared limited or dependent on histology, and review articles do not regard pazopanib as a preferred option here. The mechanistic link exists, but efficacy is uncertain.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindication signals from the knowledge graph** (these are graph-derived associations, not label text): hypertension, long QT syndrome and torsades de pointes, liver failure and drug-induced liver injury, hypothyroidism, myocardial infarction, intracerebral hemorrhage, active peptic ulcer disease, pancreatitis, proteinuria, diarrhoeal disease, hyperbilirubinaemia (including Gilbert syndrome), hypercalcaemia and nephrocalcinosis, potassium deficiency, phosphorus metabolism disorders, and haemoglobinopathy.
- **Drug interactions:** 100 interactions were found in total, and 20 are listed in the data compiled for this report.
  - **Major:** the acid-suppressing drugs famotidine, ranitidine, cimetidine, omeprazole, rabeprazole, esomeprazole and dexlansoprazole; clarithromycin; and dolasetron.
  - **Moderate:** pioglitazone, saxagliptin, loperamide, aprepitant, dexamethasone, budesonide (including nasal), bisacodyl, magnesium oxide, polyethylene glycol with electrolytes, and eliglustat.

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available, so please refer to the package insert for authoritative safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Liposarcoma has the most developed evidence (L2: completed Phase 2 trials), but the data show limited, histology-dependent activity and no randomized evidence of benefit. Pazopanib's New Zealand licences are both cancelled, and the package insert safety review has not been done, which is a blocking gap. The liposarcoma prediction is best treated as a research question.

**Other predictions in the dataset:**
- **Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans** (L2, Research Question): strong mechanistic rationale through COL1A1-PDGFB and PDGFR, with a multicenter Phase 2 study (PMID 32956651) and a terminated Phase 2a trial ([NCT01059656](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01059656), n=23). Imatinib remains the established therapy, so pazopanib is a second-line consideration.
- **Fibroblastic neoplasm** (L2, Research Question): includes a Phase 2 trial in solitary fibrous tumour and extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma ([NCT02066285](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02066285), n=96). The category is heterogeneous, so results cannot be generalised.
- **Unclassified renal cell carcinoma** (L3, Research Question): retrospective series and a single-arm open-label study (PMID 28546525), with more modest response than in clear cell disease.
- **Hold (L5, prediction only):** Xp11.2/TFE3-translocation renal cell carcinoma, renal cell carcinoma associated with neuroblastoma, childhood kidney cell carcinoma, ovarian myxoid liposarcoma, heart fibrosarcoma and kidney fibrosarcoma.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Download and review the Medsafe package insert (warnings and contraindications), the blocking gap.
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank.
- Published results and outcomes of the liposarcoma trials (NCT01506596, NCT01692496) to quantify efficacy.
- Clarification of New Zealand supply and regulatory status, since both licences are cancelled.
- A safety monitoring plan covering cardiovascular, hepatic and thyroid risks and interactions with acid-suppressing drugs.
