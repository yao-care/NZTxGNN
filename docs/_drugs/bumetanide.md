## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Bumetanide is, however, a well-established loop diuretic. It inhibits the Na-K-2Cl cotransporter (NKCC2) in the thick ascending limb of the loop of Henle, producing rapid sodium and water excretion.

In acute pulmonary heart disease (acute cor pulmonale), the right ventricle is overloaded and venous congestion develops. Reducing preload and extracellular fluid volume could plausibly relieve this congestion. This shares the volume-overload pathophysiology of heart failure, for which loop diuretics are commonly used.

There is an important caution. The right ventricle in acute cor pulmonale (for example, in acute pulmonary embolism) depends on preload. Aggressive diuresis may therefore lower cardiac output and cause harm. The TxGNN score is a computational prediction and is not clinical evidence.

## Safety Considerations

- **Conditions listed as contraindications in the data compiled for this report:** liver disease, gallbladder disease, thrombocytopenia, potassium deficiency, diabetes mellitus, diabetic ketoacidosis, hyperuricaemia, hepatic coma, hepatic encephalopathy, anuria, hypercalcaemia, nephrocalcinosis, phosphorus metabolism disease, hearing disorder and auditory system disease. These do not come from label text, so confirm them against the package insert.
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were found. Those listed include moderate-level interactions with:
  - acarbose, metformin, alogliptin and albiglutide (antidiabetic agents)
  - hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, triamcinolone and beclomethasone (corticosteroids)
  - amphotericin B (including lipid and cholesteryl sulfate forms)
  - omeprazole, rabeprazole, bupropion, morphine, bisacodyl and acetohexamide
  
  Minor-level interactions include doxycycline and acetylsalicylic acid.
- **Haemodynamic caution:** the preload-dependent right ventricle in acute cor pulmonale may not tolerate aggressive diuresis.

Please refer to the package insert for the full warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
- No registered trial or publication directly studies bumetanide in acute pulmonary heart disease, so the evidence level is L5. The high TxGNN score is not clinical evidence, and diuresis may be harmful in a preload-dependent right ventricle.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Clinical studies or case series of loop diuretics in acute cor pulmonale, including the effect on right ventricular function and cardiac output
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from Medsafe
- Mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- A safety plan for patients with electrolyte disturbance, renal impairment, hearing disorder or diabetes, and for those taking interacting drugs
