## Daunorubicin: anthracycline topoisomerase II inhibitor → Hodgkins lymphoma
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Daunorubicin is an anthracycline chemotherapy drug that damages DNA and blocks topoisomerase II in rapidly dividing cancer cells.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **Hodgkins lymphoma**, with a score of 0.9981.
Direct support is limited: **2 Phase 2 trials** (both multi-drug regimens, one in non-Hodgkin lymphoma) and **literature that mostly concerns related lymphomas or general anthracycline use**.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Daunorubicin intercalates into DNA and inhibits topoisomerase II. Detailed mechanism-of-action data were not available in the data compiled for this report, so this description reflects the general known pharmacology of the anthracycline class.

Doxorubicin, a closely related anthracycline, is a core component of the standard ABVD regimen for Hodgkin lymphoma. That gives a plausible mechanistic reason why daunorubicin could be active in the same disease.

However, this is an indirect argument. The supporting trials are multi-agent regimens, so the contribution of daunorubicin alone cannot be separated, and none of the studies directly examines daunorubicin in Hodgkin lymphoma. Because no cited study addresses Hodgkin lymphoma itself, the evidence level is capped at L5 (model prediction with only indirect support).

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is high (0.9981) and the mechanism is plausible given the use of a related anthracycline in standard Hodgkin lymphoma regimens. However, no cited study directly examines daunorubicin in Hodgkin lymphoma, and the trials found are multi-drug regimens, partly in non-Hodgkin lymphoma.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Studies that directly assess daunorubicin in Hodgkin lymphoma, ideally separating its contribution from the rest of a regimen
- Comparison against the established ABVD regimen
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from Medsafe
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data
- A cardiac and haematological safety monitoring plan
