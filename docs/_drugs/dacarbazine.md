# Dacarbazine: From Hodgkin Lymphoma and Metastatic Melanoma to Upper Aerodigestive Tract Neoplasm

## One-Sentence Summary

Dacarbazine is a cytotoxic chemotherapy agent originally used for metastatic melanoma and Hodgkin lymphoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **upper aerodigestive tract neoplasm**,
but **no clinical trials** are registered for this indication and the **20 publications** found are only indirectly related, so evidence is limited to the model prediction.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general pharmacological knowledge, dacarbazine is a DNA-alkylating agent. It is a methylating prodrug activated in the liver to MTIC, which damages the DNA of rapidly dividing cells. This mechanism has been proven in melanoma and Hodgkin lymphoma.

A cytotoxic mechanism could plausibly act on tumours of the upper aerodigestive tract, such as squamous cell carcinomas. However, no direct mechanistic or clinical data support this. The very high TxGNN score is a model prediction only.

The related literature concerns neighbouring but distinct conditions: mucosal melanoma of the head and neck, mucosal metastases of melanoma, Hodgkin lymphoma at head and neck sites, head and neck paragangliomas, and medullary thyroid carcinoma (dacarbazine plus 5-FU). These are indirect evidence and do not establish efficacy in upper aerodigestive tract neoplasms.

## Safety Considerations

- **Disease-related contraindications (from the drug knowledge graph, not label text)**: kidney disease, liver disease, gallbladder disease, pyoureter, thrombocytopenia, anaemia (including macrocytic anaemia), leukopenia, hepatic vein thrombosis, Budd-Chiari syndrome.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found. Those with a stated severity:

  - Levofloxacin (Minor)
  - Others, such as pantoprazole, omeprazole, morphine, metformin, prednisone, ondansetron and aprepitant, have no severity grading and should be reviewed individually.

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests on a model score alone (L4). There are no registered trials, and the literature covers only related conditions such as mucosal melanoma and medullary thyroid carcinoma. In addition, both New Zealand licences are cancelled and the package insert safety data have not been reviewed.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Direct clinical evidence of dacarbazine in upper aerodigestive tract neoplasms (for example, trials or well-designed cohort studies)
- Confirmed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Medsafe package insert warnings and contraindications, to allow safety screening
- Clarification of the New Zealand supply situation, since no valid licence currently exists
- Comparison against current standard therapies for the target indication
