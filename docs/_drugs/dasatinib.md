## Dasatinib: Ewing sarcoma
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Dasatinib is an oral multi-targeted kinase inhibitor that blocks BCR-ABL and SRC-family kinases.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be relevant for **Ewing sarcoma**, with **2 clinical trials** and **10 listed publications** (of which only some address Ewing sarcoma directly).
The evidence level is **L3**: there is no randomized trial, and the support comes from a Phase 2 sarcoma trial that included Ewing sarcoma patients, a small case series, and preclinical work.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on the published literature, dasatinib inhibits SRC-family kinases, as well as KIT and PDGFR.

In Ewing sarcoma cell lines, SRC and the FAK-Src axis drive invadopodia formation, migration and invasion. Laboratory studies show that dasatinib has antiproliferative and antimigratory activity in Ewing sarcoma and other sarcoma cell lines (PMID 18202781, 17363602, 27566104, 31521948). This gives a plausible mechanistic link.

Clinical results are less encouraging. A review notes that single-agent dasatinib failed in the Ewing sarcoma and rhabdomyosarcoma subtypes in a Phase 2 study (PMID 35655525). Any future role would more likely be in combination regimens, such as the RIST regimen (rapamycin, irinotecan, dasatinib, temozolomide).

## Safety Considerations

- **Conditions of concern for use**: The data compiled for this report flag long QT syndrome, torsades de pointes, congestive heart failure, myocardial infarction, thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, anaemia, liver disease, active peptic ulcer disease, intracerebral haemorrhage and pericardial effusion. These come from drug-disease relation data, not from label text.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found. Major interactions include acid-reducing agents (famotidine, ranitidine, cimetidine, omeprazole, rabeprazole), dexamethasone, acetylsalicylic acid and clarithromycin. Moderate interactions include hydrocortisone, loperamide, aprepitant, budesonide, antacids (magnesium oxide, aluminium hydroxide, calcium carbonate) and several laxatives.

Please also refer to the package insert for full safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Mechanistic and preclinical support for SRC inhibition in Ewing sarcoma is reasonable, but single-agent dasatinib did not show efficacy in Ewing sarcoma in the Phase 2 sarcoma study. The only Ewing-specific combination trial ended early with 7 patients, and the case series is small and uncontrolled. There is no randomized evidence (evidence level L3).

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Ewing sarcoma-specific results from NCT00464620 and any outcome data from the RIST regimen
- A prospective trial of a dasatinib-containing combination in relapsed Ewing sarcoma
- Package insert safety information (warnings and contraindications) and detailed mechanism of action data
- A cardiac and haematological monitoring plan, plus a review of acid-suppressant co-medication
