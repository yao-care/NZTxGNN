## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for dasatinib is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on the published literature, dasatinib inhibits SRC-family kinases and the FAK-Src signalling axis, as well as c-KIT and PDGFR.

In Ewing sarcoma cell lines, SRC activation drives invadopodia formation, cell migration and invasion. Laboratory studies report that dasatinib has antiproliferative and antimigratory activity in Ewing sarcoma and neuroblastoma cell lines, and induces apoptosis in bone sarcoma cells that depend on SRC. This makes the link biologically plausible.

Clinical results are less encouraging. A review notes that in a Phase 2 study of advanced sarcomas including Ewing sarcoma, dasatinib failed as a single agent in this subtype. Interest has therefore moved toward combinations, such as the RIST regimen (rapamycin, irinotecan, dasatinib, temozolomide), reported in a small descriptive case series in relapsed Ewing sarcoma.

**Evidence level note:** Two trials are registered, but one is a broad multi-sarcoma study whose Ewing-specific results cannot be confirmed from the data, and the other was terminated after 7 patients with no efficacy conclusion. No completed trial directly establishes efficacy in Ewing sarcoma, so the evidence level is set at L5 rather than the higher level that a simple count might suggest.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is very high (0.9990), and the SRC/FAK mechanism is supported by laboratory studies. However, no study directly demonstrates clinical benefit in Ewing sarcoma: the single-agent Phase 2 experience was reported as unsuccessful in this subtype, and the Ewing-specific combination trial ended with 7 patients. The evidence therefore remains at L5.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Ewing-specific results from the multi-sarcoma Phase 2 trial (NCT00464620)
- Prospective clinical data for dasatinib-containing combinations, such as the RIST regimen, in relapsed Ewing sarcoma
- Mechanism-of-action data and package insert warnings and contraindications for a formal safety screen
- A cardiac, haematological and drug-interaction monitoring plan, given the long list of cautions
