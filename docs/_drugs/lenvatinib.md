## One-Sentence Summary

Lenvatinib is an oral multi-kinase inhibitor (VEGFR, FGFR, PDGFRα, RET, KIT) that has been marketed in New Zealand as Lenvima capsules.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **liposarcoma**, with a score of 0.9951.
Support comes from **1 completed single-arm Phase Ib/II trial** and **1 matching publication** (evidence level **L3**), with no randomized trial.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Lenvatinib blocks several receptor tyrosine kinases, including VEGFR1-3, FGFR1-4, PDGFRα, RET and KIT. These pathways drive tumour blood-vessel growth and growth signalling, which are also relevant in soft tissue sarcomas such as liposarcoma. Detailed mechanism of action data were not available in the data compiled for this report, so this description reflects the drug's generally known targets.

Preclinical work suggests that eribulin, the partner drug used in the clinical study, works synergistically with agents that have different mechanisms. A completed single-arm Phase Ib/II study of lenvatinib plus eribulin in advanced liposarcoma and leiomyosarcoma gives a direct clinical signal. However, it has no randomized comparator and tests a combination, so it cannot isolate lenvatinib's own contribution.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
The only direct evidence is one single-arm Phase Ib/II study of lenvatinib plus eribulin (NCT03526679; PMID 36129471). It supports evidence level L3, but there is no randomized trial and the effect of lenvatinib alone cannot be separated.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Randomized or comparative data in liposarcoma, ideally separating lenvatinib's contribution from eribulin
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from Medsafe
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- A safety monitoring plan that takes the major drug interactions into account
