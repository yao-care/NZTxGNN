## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 326 known interactions are recorded, all of the ones listed here at moderate level. Examples include:
  - Sympathomimetics: epinephrine (including ophthalmic and topical forms), ephedrine, phentermine, diethylpropion and fenfluramine
  - QT-relevant agents: clarithromycin, cisapride, dolasetron, palonosetron and granisetron
  - Others: famotidine, loperamide, canagliflozin, ertugliflozin, semaglutide and albiglutide
- **Class consideration**: LABA monotherapy is not recommended in asthma because of safety concerns. Concomitant beta-blockers, QT-prolonging agents and other sympathomimetics should be reviewed.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.
