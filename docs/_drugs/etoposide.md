# Etoposide: From Small Cell Lung Carcinoma and Germ Cell Tumours to Ewing Sarcoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Etoposide is a topoisomerase II inhibitor chemotherapy originally used for small cell lung carcinoma and testicular germ cell tumours (including seminoma, yolk sac tumour and teratoma).
The TxGNN model's top-ranked prediction is well-differentiated fetal adenocarcinoma of the lung, but the best-supported prediction is **Ewing sarcoma**, with **33 clinical trials** and **19 publications** (including several randomised phase 3 trials) currently supporting this direction.
This report uses Ewing sarcoma as the lead indication because the top-ranked prediction has only a single case report and no registered trials.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, etoposide inhibits topoisomerase II, causing DNA double-strand breaks in rapidly dividing tumour cells. Its efficacy in small cell lung carcinoma and germ cell tumours is established.

Ewing sarcoma is a highly proliferative paediatric and young-adult bone and soft-tissue cancer that is notably sensitive to genotoxic agents such as etoposide. Ifosfamide plus etoposide (IE), alternating with vincristine, doxorubicin and cyclophosphamide (VDC), is an established regimen supported by randomised phase 3 trials and consensus guidance. A 2018 mechanistic paper reports that EWS-FLI1 causes R-loops and blocks BRCA1 repair, which may explain this sensitivity.

A similar rationale applies to rhabdomyosarcoma (rank 6, also L1), where ifosfamide/etoposide has shown activity in metastatic and intermediate-risk disease. The remaining predictions are weaker. Most rest on case reports (pulmonary blastoma, fetal adenocarcinoma, vaginal and prostate rhabdomyosarcoma) or have no direct evidence (the two bile duct rhabdomyosarcomas). The primary pulmonary lymphoma prediction is supported only by literature on other lymphoma subtypes.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications / risk conditions (knowledge-graph associations, not label text)**: thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, agranulocytosis, anaemia (including macrocytic), leukopenia, interstitial lung disease, pulmonary fibrosis, impaired renal function, acidosis/metabolic acidosis and drug-induced liver injury.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were identified. Moderate-level examples include aprepitant, rolapitant, metronomidazole, tinidazole, eliglustat, rosuvastatin and simvastatin. Levofloxacin is minor. Others (e.g. cimetidine, pantoprazole, morphine, doxycycline) have an unknown severity level. A full interaction review is needed before use.
- Guardrails noted for the Ewing sarcoma regimen: myelosuppression, secondary leukaemia risk, cumulative dose and DDI review.

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the package insert for complete safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails** (for Ewing sarcoma and rhabdomyosarcoma). **Hold** for the other predictions, including the rank 1 prediction (fetal adenocarcinoma of the lung), which has no registered trials and only one case report.

**Rationale:**
- Randomised phase 3 trials and consensus guidance support etoposide-containing regimens (IE alternating with VDC) in Ewing sarcoma. Because these are combination regimens, the independent contribution of etoposide is not isolated.
- Etoposide is already established oncology practice, so this is largely a confirmation of existing use rather than a new finding.
- No New Zealand licence is currently valid, which is a barrier to local use.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Medsafe package insert warnings and contraindications (a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Confirmation of local supply or licensing, since all New Zealand licences are cancelled
- A myelosuppression and secondary malignancy monitoring plan, plus a full DDI review
- For the lower-ranked predictions, disease-specific trial or cohort data before any further consideration
