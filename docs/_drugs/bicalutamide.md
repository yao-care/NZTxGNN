## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, bicalutamide blocks the androgen receptor (AR). Androgen signalling influences hair follicle activity, so AR blockade is a plausible way to modulate androgen-dependent hair growth.

The one publication retrieved is a comment on a retrospective review of bicalutamide used for minoxidil-induced hypertrichosis in female pattern hair loss. This concerns drug-induced hypertrichosis, not congenital or generalized forms of the disease, and it is a commentary rather than a study of the predicted disease itself. The mechanistic link is therefore plausible but indirect, and the evidence level is capped at L5.
