## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for benzatropine are not available in the data compiled for this report. The pharmacology records list these targets:

- M1 muscarinic receptor (rat)
- M2 muscarinic receptor (rat)
- Dopamine transporter, DAT (rat)
- B0AT1 amino acid transporter (human)

This matches its known profile as a muscarinic antagonist with dopamine reuptake inhibition.

PLA2G6-associated neurodegeneration includes infantile neuroaxonal dystrophy and PLA2G6-related dystonia-parkinsonism. Some forms involve parkinsonism and dystonia. These motor features are the kind that anticholinergic drugs may ease. This could be the basis of the model's prediction.

The link is unverified, and the benefit would likely be limited to symptoms. Benzatropine would not be expected to modify the underlying disease, which involves impaired phospholipid remodelling and iron and lipid dysregulation. The score is high, but it comes from the model alone.
