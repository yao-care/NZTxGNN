## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action text is not available in the data compiled for this report. Pharmacology records show that icatibant is a peptide ligand that acts on the bradykinin B2 receptor (BDKRB2) and also has activity at the B1 receptor (BDKRB1). It reduces signalling by bradykinin, a vasoactive peptide.

The link to this disease is only a computational prediction. Serpinopathies arise when serpin proteins misfold and polymerize into toxic aggregates. The model's high score likely reflects network proximity between serpin biology and the bradykinin pathway. Icatibant acts downstream, at a receptor, and does not address serpin misfolding or polymerization. A direct mechanistic rationale is therefore weak, and the score alone should not be read as evidence of benefit.
