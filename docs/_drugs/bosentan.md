## Bosentan: dual endothelin receptor antagonist → rheumatoid arthritis

## One-Sentence Summary

Bosentan is a dual endothelin receptor antagonist (blocking both ETA and ETB receptors). The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **rheumatoid arthritis**, with a score of 0.9980. The compiled data contain **no clinical trials** for this disease, and the **literature** consists mainly of animal studies and reviews of related conditions, so the evidence is weak.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Bosentan blocks endothelin receptors. Endothelin signalling contributes to inflammation in the joint lining, to vascular dysfunction and to the release of inflammatory mediators such as TNF and LTB4. Detailed mechanism-of-action data for bosentan are not available in the data compiled for this report, so this reasoning relies on the published preclinical literature.

In a mouse model of collagen-induced arthritis, bosentan reduced joint inflammation (PMID 22249931). In a separate mouse model, endothelin receptor blockade also modulated zymosan-induced arthritis (PMID 18515326). These findings give a plausible mechanistic rationale, but they come from animals only. No human studies of bosentan in rheumatoid arthritis were identified.

Much of the other retrieved literature concerns pulmonary hypertension associated with connective tissue disease. That is a different condition and does not directly address rheumatoid arthritis.

## Clinical Trial Evidence

Currently no related clinical trials registered.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were recorded. The 20 listed in the data are all of moderate severity. They include corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, triamcinolone, dexamethasone, betamethasone), antidiabetic agents (pioglitazone, acetohexamide, chlorpropamide, canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, ertugliflozin), and CYP-interacting drugs (clarithromycin, cimetidine, metronidazole, aprepitant, clotrimazole). This is relevant in rheumatoid arthritis, where corticosteroids are often co-prescribed.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications, as these were not available in the data compiled for this report.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is very high, but the support is limited to two animal studies. There are no clinical trials and no human studies in rheumatoid arthritis, so the evidence level is L5.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Human clinical data (for example, a pilot trial) of bosentan in rheumatoid arthritis
- Mechanism-of-action data and package insert warnings and contraindications
- A review of the interaction burden with corticosteroids and other drugs commonly used in rheumatoid arthritis, and of the liver and haematological safety profile
