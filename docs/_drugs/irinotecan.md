# Irinotecan: From Metastatic Colon Cancer and Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma to Female Breast Carcinoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Irinotecan is a topoisomerase I inhibitor chemotherapy, originally used for metastatic colon cancer and pancreatic adenocarcinoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **female breast carcinoma**, but the supporting evidence is mostly early-phase: **9 clinical trials** (only 2 directly relevant, both Phase 1) and **20 publications**, none showing Phase 3 results for irinotecan itself in breast cancer.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the compiled data. Based on known pharmacology, irinotecan targets DNA topoisomerase I, and its active metabolite SN-38 causes DNA damage in rapidly dividing cells. This mechanism is not specific to one tumour type. The target mapping rests on structural similarity to camptothecin and topotecan rather than direct affinity data.

The strongest mechanistic support comes from sacituzumab govitecan, an anti-Trop-2 antibody-drug conjugate that delivers SN-38. It has been studied in metastatic breast cancer, including the Phase III TROPiCS-02 trial in HR+/HER2- disease. This supports the activity of the SN-38 payload in breast cancer but does not directly validate irinotecan itself.

Earlier literature describes irinotecan as having only marginal activity in breast cancer, with a rationale for sequential use with mitomycin, which upregulates topoisomerase I. The TxGNN score is a computational prediction, not clinical evidence.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The evidence consists of small Phase 1 studies and indirect data from SN-38 antibody-drug conjugates, with no Phase 3 results for irinotecan in breast cancer. Irinotecan has also been described as having only marginal activity in this disease. All New Zealand licences are cancelled, and package insert safety data are missing. At this stage it is a research question rather than an actionable repurposing candidate.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Medsafe package insert warnings and contraindications (blocking gap for safety screening)
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Results of the PHENOMENAL Phase 2 study and any randomised data on irinotecan or liposomal irinotecan in breast cancer
- Clarification of the regulatory and supply route, since no valid New Zealand licence currently exists
