## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Chlorambucil is known as an alkylating agent: it cross-links DNA and so interferes with the division of malignant lymphoid cells. It has long been used in B-cell lymphoid malignancies.

The predicted disease is a subtype of chronic lymphocytic leukemia/small lymphocytic lymphoma (CLL/SLL), the unmutated-IGHV, pre-germinal-centre type. It belongs to the same broad family of mature B-cell neoplasms in which alkylators are used, so the prediction is biologically plausible.

The only supporting paper is a 2003 Dutch-language review on risk-adapted management of CLL. It describes the two CLL subtypes but is not a study of chlorambucil in this subtype. Because no cited study directly examines chlorambucil in this specific disease, the evidence level is L5, and the high score should be read as a model prediction, not clinical proof.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is very high, but no trials are registered and the only paper is a general CLL review. Without direct evidence for this subtype, the prediction cannot yet support a repurposing decision.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Studies of chlorambucil specifically in unmutated-IGHV (pre-germinal-centre) CLL/SLL, and comparison with current standard therapies
- Mechanism-of-action data
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from Medsafe
- Clarification of the licence status of Leukeran FC and its approved indication
- A haematological monitoring and handling plan for a cytotoxic agent
