## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Ethinylestradiol is a synthetic estrogen found in combined contraceptive products in New Zealand, such as those paired with norethisterone, levonorgestrel or desogestrel.

Estrogens are reported to influence circulating trace-element levels. For example, they can raise serum copper and ceruloplasmin and alter zinc distribution through changes in binding proteins such as albumin and alpha-2-macroglobulin. This is a plausible but unverified biological connection, and the direction of any effect on plasma zinc is uncertain.

Elevated plasma zinc is a laboratory finding rather than a typical treatment target, so no therapeutic rationale is evident. The high TxGNN score (rank 2283) should be treated as a model output only, not as evidence of benefit.
